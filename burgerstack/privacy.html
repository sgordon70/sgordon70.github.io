BurgerStack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

BurgerStack does not collect any data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your best Order Rush scores are saved on your iPhone so you can try to beat them. They are never uploaded.
  • Share cards are created on your iPhone. They only leave it if you choose to share or save one yourself.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no personal information
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No location, camera, contacts or photo library access
  • No data is ever sold or shared with third parties

Changes to this policy

If BurgerStack's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@blackhookgroup.com, or see the Support page.
