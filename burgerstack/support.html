Common questions

How do I serve a burger in Order Rush?
On iPhone Duo, fold the phone shut. The outer screen shows the customer's verdict, and opening the phone brings the next customer. On other iPhones, tap the Serve button.

How are orders scored?
You earn points for having the right ingredients, stacked in the right order, and a tip for speed. Perfect orders in a row grow your tip multiplier, up to double.

My order says "messy". What happened?
One or more layers are in the wrong place compared with the ticket. Tap the stack button (the layers icon) to drag layers into the right order before you serve.

How do I remove an ingredient?
Press and hold its card and choose Remove one, or open the stack and tap the red minus button.

What is the Daily Special?
One order per day, the same for every player. Try it as often as you like to beat your best score for the day.
