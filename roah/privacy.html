Privacy Policy

A plain description of what Roah collects, why, and what stays on your device.

Last updated August 24, 2026

In short

  • The pages you scan never leave your device. Roah reads text on-device; no photo or page image is ever uploaded.
  • What syncs is small: which words you tapped or replayed, grouped by category (like "name" or "long word") — not full sentences or page content.
  • You don't need an account to use Roah. The camera, on-device text recognition, and word-highlighting — what we call Core Mode — work entirely on your phone, with no internet connection and nothing sent anywhere. Signing in only adds two things: syncing your struggle profile across your devices, and confirming an active Roah+ subscription.
  • We don't sell data, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers in Roah.

01 What we collect

Roah's difficulty engine and personalization only need to know which kinds of words trip you up — not the pages you're reading. The data below is what actually syncs to Roah's backend when you're signed in.

DataWhat it includesWhy
Word interaction history A tapped word, its category (e.g. "proper noun," "irregular spelling"), the action (tapped, replayed, or skipped), and a timestamp Builds the "struggle profile" that quietly adjusts which words get highlighted for you over time
Struggle profile A per-category score derived from the history above, which decays over time Drives predictive highlighting — the core feature of the app
Subscription status Platform (iOS), product, status, and expiration date Confirms your Roah+ entitlement without re-checking the App Store every time

We don't collect your name, contacts, location, or anything from outside the app itself.

02 What stays on your device Never synced

Every photo you capture and every word Roah recognizes is processed with Apple's on-device Vision framework. That image, and the full text of the page, never leaves your phone — not to Roah's backend, not to Apple, not to anyone. Only the word/category-level tap data described above is ever sent anywhere, and only once you're signed in.

Core Mode — the camera, OCR, and difficulty highlighting — works completely offline. Signing in only adds cross-device sync and Roah+ entitlement checks on top of that.

03 Optional AI explanations

If you choose to paste your own Anthropic API key in Settings, tapping a word can request a plain-language explanation. That request goes directly from your device to Anthropic using your own key — it never passes through Roah's servers, and Roah never sees or stores your key or the words you've asked about. This feature is entirely optional and off by default.

04 Who we share data with

PartyWhat they see
Our database provider (Google Firebase) Word/category interaction history, struggle profile, and subscription status — protected so only you can access your own data
Apple Standard App Store purchase and device information Apple collects for any app, used to validate your Roah+ subscription
Anthropic (only if you enable AI explanations) The word you tapped and its surrounding sentence, sent directly using your own API key

We don't use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't sell or rent any data to anyone.

05 Retention & deletion

Word interaction history is kept only as long as needed to compute your struggle profile, which itself decays automatically over time. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any point by contacting us below — we'll remove it within 30 days.

06 Children's privacy

Roah is intended for general readers and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

07 Changes to this policy

If what Roah collects changes in a future update, we'll update this page and adjust the "last updated" date above.
