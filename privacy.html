DropQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

DropQuest is designed to collect as little data as possible, and to be upfront about the data it does handle.

What we collect when you create a quest

  • Stop locations and hint photos — when you build a quest, the GPS coordinates and photos for each stop are stored as part of that quest. By default, this data is uploaded to Apple's CloudKit service so the quest can be opened via a shared link. If that upload isn't possible, DropQuest instead shares the quest as a file directly between devices (AirDrop, Messages, etc.), with no server involved.

What we collect while you're playing a quest

  • Your live location — used only on your own device to detect when you've arrived at the next stop. This is never uploaded anywhere.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no personal information
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data is ever sold or shared with third parties

Data retention and access

A quest you send remains stored in our CloudKit database until you delete it from DropQuest's Home screen, or if we remove it after a report of inappropriate content. Access to this data is limited to what's needed to operate DropQuest and respond to reports — it is never sold, shared, or used for anything else.

Changes to this policy

If DropQuest's data practices change in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through DropQuest's Support link on its App Store page.
