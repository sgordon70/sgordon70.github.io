DropQuest Support

DropQuest is a scavenger hunt app — one person (the host) picks a few real spots, snaps a photo hint at each, and sends the quest to a friend to go find.

Contact us

Questions, bugs, or anything else — email hello@blackhookgroup.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

A stop won't unlock even though I'm there.

Sending a quest is stuck or slow.

I want to report a quest.
While playing a hunt, tap "Report this quest" at the bottom of the screen, or email us directly at the address above with the quest link.

I want a sent quest removed.
On the Home screen, find the quest under "Quests You've Sent" and tap the trash icon to delete it.
